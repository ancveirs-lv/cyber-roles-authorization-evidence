# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participants must discuss terminology, evidence, and sources without harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or personal degradation.

Constructive behaviour includes:

- distinguishing a factual correction from a judgement about a person's identity;
- citing the source and scope of a definition;
- acknowledging legitimate disagreement between communities and institutions;
- correcting mistakes transparently;
- protecting private, sensitive, and incident-related information.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- publishing personal data or credentials;
- encouraging unauthorised testing;
- using the repository's editorial metaphors to target or harass a named person;
- knowingly presenting a community term as a legal or institutional standard;
- repeated bad-faith disruption after a maintainer warning.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to enforce this standard. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and applied proportionately.
